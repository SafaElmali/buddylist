The app's real interface in demo mode: the replies are scripted, not from a real agent.

  • Double-click a contact and say anything
  • Say fix, plan, ask me, diagram, subagents or background for other replies
  • Esc nudges it while it works
  • Terminal (⌘J): a pretend shell, try git status
  • The + after its tab opens Changes, with diffs
  • The tags on a contact are its pull request
  • ... by the search box groups, filters and sorts the list, or adds a project
  • Invite an agent, then Conversation > Effort > Let the agents decide
