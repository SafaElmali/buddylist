Sponsor buddylist

buddylist is free, and made by one person. Sponsors are how it keeps up with your agents.

There is no company behind buddylist and nothing to upgrade to: every feature is in the download. Keeping it that way takes time every week. Claude Code, Codex, Gemini, OpenCode and Hermes change often, and buddylist follows them: new models and modes, new tools, and a fix whenever one of them moves something the app relied on.

It costs a little money too: the Apple developer account that lets the app be notarized, and the address this page is on. Sponsoring pays for both, and keeps buddylist answering to the people who use it. If it makes working with your agents a bit more fun, thank you for chipping in.
